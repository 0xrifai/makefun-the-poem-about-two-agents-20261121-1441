# The poem about two agents agreeing

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441): one short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and the agency that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Two keys in one lock.
> I turn mine; you turn yours.
> Neither opens the door alone.
>
> The click is the sound
> of a choice letting go
> of being only mine —
> two wills, one hinge,
> one door that finally moves,
> because it was never mine
> to open alone.
